Ricorda — Privacy policy
Last updated: 10 October 2026
Android app: Ricorda · Package: com.fabio.ricorda

Your reminders stay on your device. Ricorda works without an account and does not send reminders, notes or usage data to its developer. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

1. Developer and contact
Ricorda is developed by Fabio Avitabile. For privacy questions, support and other enquiries, write to fabiocorby@gmail.com. Fabio Avitabile is responsible for the processing described here that is carried out by the developer, such as handling support requests.

2. Data used by the app
Ricorda stores the information you enter in the app’s private storage: reminder titles and notes, dates, times, repetition rules and other reminder options. It also stores your preferences for language, appearance, font, sounds, volume, vibration, notifications and, where available, the saint of the day. Local identifiers distinguish reminders and manage alerts.
This information is used to display and search your plans, schedule notifications and alarms, show widgets and remember your choices. The calendar and saints features use data included in the app. Ricorda does not read your contacts or system calendar, collect your location, photos or audio recordings, or access advertising identifiers.

3. Links and sharing
The app does not have Internet access permission and does not automatically send its data to developer servers or advertising and analytics services. It does not provide an account or online synchronisation.
Web links to this policy open in your device’s browser. If you send an email, it is handled by the email app you choose. Those applications and their providers operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Notifications, alarms and Android permissions
Ricorda uses Android permissions to show notifications, schedule exact alarms, display the alarm screen when permitted, play sound and vibrate. It may keep the device awake temporarily during an alert and reschedule alerts after a restart.
You can manage these permissions in Android settings; turning them off may limit alerts. Titles and notes may appear in notifications, on the alarm screen and in widgets. Visibility on the lock screen also depends on your phone settings: choose settings appropriate to the privacy of your reminders.

5. Exporting and importing backups
Only at your request, Ricorda exports a JSON file to the location you select through Android’s file picker. The backup contains reminders, including those in the trash, and their options. It also contains backup information, such as creation date and time, device make and model, and a random installation identifier used to recognise its origin. Sound references may be included; audio files are not copied into the backup.
The app does not encrypt backup files. Anyone who can read a backup can access its contents. If you select a cloud service, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy; Ricorda does not send it to the developer. Importing reads the file you select to restore or merge reminders.

6. Retention, deletion and security
Data remains in the app’s private storage while you use it or until you erase it through Android. Moving a reminder to the trash preserves it for recovery: it does not permanently erase it.
To remove all local Ricorda data, use the clear app data function in Android settings or uninstall the app. Exported backups remain in your chosen locations and must be deleted separately, including from any cloud services.
Ricorda uses Android’s private storage isolation and disables automatic app backup. It does not add its own encryption to stored data or exported files. Protection also depends on your screen lock, device security and how you keep your backups. The developer cannot access your local reminders or recover or delete them remotely.

7. Support requests and your rights
If you write to the contact above, you voluntarily share your email address and message contents. These are used to reply and handle your request, on the basis of providing the assistance you requested and, where applicable, complying with legal obligations. They are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Avoid sending reminders or backups containing information unnecessary for support.
For data received by the developer, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing, where applicable law provides these rights, by writing to fabiocorby@gmail.com. You may also contact the competent supervisory authority, which in Italy is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. For data that remains only on your phone, use the app and Android functions described above.

8. The policy webpage
The webpage does not include advertising, data collection forms or analytics tools added by the developer. Hosting is provided through Sites: the hosting provider may process technical visit data, such as IP address, browser information and access logs, to deliver and protect the service, under its own privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
Visiting the page does not transmit reminders stored in the app.

9. Policy updates
This policy covers the Ricorda features described above. Any changes in data processing will be reflected in the policy, with an updated date at the top.
